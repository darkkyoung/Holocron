## Remaining limits / decision

Integrated local prototype is runnable; **not a Production release**. Native WebGL/mobile-device performance remains unverified. Spatial shortcut branches and full pit-lane entry/exit are not implemented: inherited shortcut events affect authoritative speed and pit stops use roadside offsets. Art/evidence is deliberately temporary. There is no season service, admin UI, settlement, or Production `/racing` page. Regular/custom season decisions are recorded in RACING_DESIGN, not silently implemented.

40,000 seeds are evidence, not proof of perfect balance. Ordinary two-car eliminations occur in 26.0625% of races, and drafting crossing counts include repeated battles. These need ordinary human playtesting, not a claim that every tuning target is final.

Production deployments 0; Production DB/Migration/Restore 0; Credit writes/settlements 0; AI/Provider calls 0. No Production state was re-read or claimed freshly verified. Canonical main remains untouched.
